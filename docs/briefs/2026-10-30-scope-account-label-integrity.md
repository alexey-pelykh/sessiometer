# Scope Brief: Account Label Integrity

Every verdict, decision and default in this brief is pipeline-authored and ratification-pending. The operator's words that bear on this run's content are the question above and "/scope the gaps discovered". ## Problem

The operator names an account by its label, and labels are not unique by ratified design (R-6 of the migration PRD). The CLI `capture` and `login`, the socket `capture` and `config-set` can each make one label name a second account without saying so. A `login` can rename a different account than the one the operator meant, and its reply cannot show it. One credential can sit under two roster entries unnoticed. Every surface downstream of the roster identifies an account by its label alone. So two accounts that share a label look the same on the status wire, in the panel, in usage history and on swap lines. This run turned the gaps the investigation found into a PRD, a design, feature stubs and tracked items. None of that work enforces label uniqueness, which the migration PRD's AC-6 forbids.
